#### Dissonant Whispers
*1st-level enchantment*
{{pen,color:red  1}}
**Casting Time:** :: 1 action
**Range:**        :: 60 feet
**Components:**   ::V
**Duration:**     :: Insta
One creature of your choice that you can see within range hears a
discordant melody in its mind. The target makes a Wisdom saving
throw. On a failed save, it takes 3d6 Psychic damage and must
immediately use its Reaction, if available, to move as far away from
you as it can, using the safest route. On a successful save, the target
takes half as much damage only.<br>
**Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot.** The damage increases by 1d6 for
each spell slot level above 1
